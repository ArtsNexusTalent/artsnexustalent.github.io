About Arts Nexus Talent

Arts Nexus Talent is an independent UK talent agency representing emerging and established performers across stage and screen.

We represent a diverse range of performers, including children and young people, and are committed to connecting talented individuals with suitable professional opportunities.

We pride ourselves on building positive working relationships with performers, families, casting professionals and production teams, offering dedicated representation and professional guidance.

What We Do

Arts Nexus Talent sources and facilitates casting opportunities across theatre, musical theatre, television, film, commercials, dance, voice-over and live performance.

We work to identify appropriate opportunities, submit suitable performers for consideration, communicate with casting and production teams, and support clients throughout the casting and booking process.

The Nexus Sessions

Alongside our agency work, we run The Nexus Sessions, a programme of monthly casting and industry workshops designed to help young performers develop their skills, build confidence and gain valuable industry insight.
