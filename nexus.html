The Nexus Sessions is a programme of monthly casting and industry workshops based in Staffordshire, delivered by Arts Nexus Talent to support young performers in developing their skills, building confidence and gaining valuable industry insight.

Led by resident coaches and guest industry professionals, our sessions provide opportunities to explore different areas of performance, develop audition techniques and connect with professionals working across the entertainment industry.
